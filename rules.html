UNIVERSAL SERVER RULES.

These are the official rules for every Beyond the Outbreak server. Pripyat and Badlands share this rulebook; each map has its own separate Tips & Tricks guide. Rules about traders, garages, safe zones, airdrops, or AI apply whenever those features are present on a server.

Required for every server: read the rules and acknowledge them on Discord.

React with ✅ to the final official rules message in Discord after reading both parts. These rules are subject to change; stay up to date with staff announcements.

All servers / Section 01

🧍 PLAYER CONDUCT

  • Treat all players with respect. Racism, hate speech, harassment, threats, targeted bullying, and excessive toxicity are prohibited.
  • Reasonable trash talk is allowed; personal attacks and deliberate disruption are not.
  • Do not impersonate staff or intentionally ruin another player’s experience.
All servers / Section 02

🏠 BASE BUILDING & TERRITORY

  • One main base per player or group, unless staff approves an exception.
  • There is no hard base-size limit currently, but excessively large or lag-inducing builds are prohibited.
  • Build at least 1,000 meters from any trader and 500 meters from any military area.
  • A military area is any location with military assets, including buildings, tents, checkpoints, bunkers, towers, compounds, and similar structures.
  • No building inside POIs, including major landmarks, unique locations, mission areas, or other notable map locations.
  • Do not block roads, entrances, ladders, tunnels, bridges, loot locations, common routes, or access to important map areas.
All servers / Section 03

🚫 NO RAIDING / BASE GRIEFING

  • Raiding another player’s base is prohibited unless specifically authorized for an official event.
  • Do not dismantle, damage, destroy, steal from, or interfere with another player’s base.
  • Do not boost, glitch, crawl, clip, or exploit your way into a base.
  • Do not block another player’s entrance or build around their territory to interfere with access.
All servers / Section 04

🐛 CHEATING & EXPLOITS

  • No hacking, cheating, duping, glitching, exploiting, or intentionally abusing unintended mechanics.
  • Do not use broken mechanics for personal gain. Report serious exploits privately to staff.
  • Intentional duplication of items or currency may result in an immediate ban.
All servers / Section 05

💰 TRADERS & ECONOMY

  • Do not block trader areas, vehicle spawn points, or access routes, and do not abandon vehicles inside trader zones.
  • Do not exploit trader bugs, pricing errors, buy/sell loops, item duplication, or other economy exploits.
  • Report incorrect buy/sell prices rather than taking advantage of them.
All servers / Section 06

🎒 LOOT & LOOT CYCLING

  • Do not hoard excessive high-demand loot solely to prevent other players from obtaining it.
  • Do not create large piles of items that harm performance or exploit persistence, containers, dropped items, or storage mechanics.
  • Loot cycling is strictly prohibited: do not remove, throw, or relocate unwanted loot for the purpose of forcing new items to spawn.
  • Normal looting is allowed. If you do not find what you want, move on. Repeated or obvious loot cycling will result in staff action.
All servers / Section 07

🤖 AI, MUTANTS & NPCs

  • Do not deliberately exploit AI pathing, inaccessible rooftops, broken geometry, or unreachable locations to farm AI or NPCs.
  • Normal tactical positioning is allowed; trapping AI using obvious exploits is not.
All servers / Section 08

🚗 VEHICLES & GARAGE SYSTEM

  • Do not steal, destroy, or sabotage another player’s vehicle unless an official server event expressly permits it.
  • Store vehicles before logging off. Do not leave them out.
  • Vehicles left in safe zones may be deleted after 1 hour. Vehicles left out for 24 hours or more will be removed.
  • Garage storage locations: 🏳️ flag poles and 🚧 parking signs at traders. This is the established garage system and is not open to player-requested changes.
  • Do not block roads, traders, entrances, or important locations with vehicles, or use vehicles to exploit bases or map geometry.
  • No replacements for vehicles lost through leaving them out, safe-zone cleanup, crashes, DayZ physics, or player negligence.
All servers / Section 09

🪂 AIRDROP CLAIMS

  • All players must claim airdrops they intend to pursue. Respect the claim system and other players.
  • If you die while attempting to obtain a claimed drop, your claim resets and the drop becomes available to others.
  • Do not selfishly abuse the airdrop claim system or interfere with fair access to drops.
All servers / Section 10

🔴 COMBAT & SERVER EVENTS

  • Follow all event-specific instructions and rules. Official event rules may temporarily override normal server rules.
  • Do not interfere with official events unless you are participating.
  • Staff decisions during an active event are final.
All servers / Section 11

📢 STAFF, REPORTS & ENFORCEMENT

  • Follow staff instructions while an issue is being handled. Dispute staff decisions through a ticket, not public arguments.
  • Provide screenshots, clips, player names, locations, and other relevant evidence when reporting issues.
  • False or intentionally misleading reports may lead to punishment.
  • Staff will only consider item or vehicle replacement requests when valid evidence is provided; evidence does not guarantee a replacement.
  • Not every situation can be listed here. Staff may address conduct that clearly violates the spirit of fair play and these rules.
All servers / Section 12

✅ MANDATORY RULES ACKNOWLEDGMENT

  • You MUST react with ✅ to the final universal rules message in Discord to confirm that you have read and understood these rules.
  • Rules are subject to change. Players are responsible for keeping up with updates.

Place the ✅ reaction on the final universal rules message in Discord. Reading the website alone does not register your acknowledgment.
