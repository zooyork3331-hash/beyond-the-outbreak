THE RULES.

Know the expectations. Respect fellow survivors. Keep the experience enjoyable for everyone.

Server 01

DEER ISLE / PVE.

The established PvE server follows community-minded survival rules.

01 / NO PVP OR RAIDING

No player killing or raiding other players' bases.

02 / BASE LIMIT

One base per player.

03 / MILITARY BUFFER

Stay at least 500 meters away from military areas when building.

04 / TRADER BUFFER

Stay at least 1,000 meters from traders and the black market when building.

05 / PLAY FAIR

No exploiting, cheating, or deliberately interfering with other players' gameplay.

06 / RESPECT EVERYONE

Harassment and abusive behavior are not welcome in the community.
