Critical survival intelligence

EXCLUSION ZONE HAZARDS.

These hazards are part of our Pripyat server configuration. Plan your route and protection before entering.

☢ Radiation zone

REACTOR 4

1,000-meter radiation zone around Reactor 4. Use a dosimeter, appropriate protection, and a safe exit plan. Gear can remain contaminated after leaving.

☣ Persistent toxic gas

BLACK MARKET

Permanently gassed. Never assume the area is safe between contamination events. Prepare a working gas mask/filter and suitable protective gear.
