Before reaching out

COMMON QUESTIONS.

I can't connect to Pripyat. What should I check?

Make sure DayZ and your mods are up to date, then connect using 45.59.162.14:2302. Open the Pripyat mod directory for your current mod list. If the launcher shows an error, copy or screenshot the exact wording for support.

I lost a vehicle or valuable items. Will staff replace them?

Replacements are not automatic. Items or vehicles lost to player negligence, DayZ physics, crashes, or vehicle cleanup are not replaced under current rules. For other incidents, submit a support request with valid evidence. Read the universal rules.

How should I report a suspected cheater or exploit?

Report it privately through the designated Discord support method. Include proof if available. Do not publicly distribute exploit instructions or harass players you suspect of rule-breaking.

Can I appeal a staff decision?

Yes. Open a support request with a clear explanation and evidence where applicable. Please don't argue about active staff decisions in game chat or public channels.

Do these rules and support policies apply to both servers?

Yes. Universal rules cover Beyond the Outbreak servers. Map-specific tips and mod information remain on each server's own page. Rules may change, so check the current version.
