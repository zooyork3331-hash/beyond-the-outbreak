Support information

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS.

Do I have to donate to play?

No. Supporting the community is always voluntary. No contribution is required to join our public community servers or access the site's guides.

Do donors receive in-game rewards?

No. Donations here are voluntary and do not provide currency, gear, vehicles, special access, gameplay perks, or other rewards.

Can I see how much the servers cost?

We'll post a funding goal once the actual monthly costs are confirmed. Until then, no target or contribution totals are claimed.

What if I have a question before contributing?

Join the official Discord and use the Staff & Support page to find our Ticket Tool panel.
