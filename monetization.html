SERVER
MONETIZATION PLAN.

Beyond the Outbreak is a community-driven DayZ server project. This page publicly describes our proposed optional supporter program and the fairness safeguards we intend to follow. It is provided for review as part of a server monetization application.

STATUS: PROPOSED — NOT ACTIVE. Paid supporter packages are not available for purchase. No payments are being collected through this program. Any paid program will begin only after the required Bohemia Interactive approval and mod-author permissions are confirmed.

01 / Objective

KEEP THE SERVERS RUNNING.

Funds received through an approved supporter program would be used to help cover the cost of game server hosting, maintenance and backups, technical operations, and community development. Participating would be entirely optional for players on our free-access servers. Everyone would continue to be subject to the same community rules and administrative standards.

03 / Community Protection

NO PAY-TO-WIN. NO EXCEPTIONS.

What we will NOT sell

  • In-game money, weapons, ammunition, or supplies
  • Vehicles, buildings, property, bases, or extra storage
  • Skill boosts, increased stats, or better loot chances
  • Trader discounts, economy advantages, or special spawn gear
  • Special treatment from staff or exemptions from rules

Equal player experience

  • Standard access to the server is free
  • No gameplay systems are locked behind supporter tiers
  • Supporters and non-supporters follow identical rules
  • Staff support and tickets remain available to everyone
  • Cosmetic recognition does not alter game balance

We will respect Bohemia Interactive's DayZ server monetization rules. We will also review the monetization rights and required permissions for all third-party server mods before activating any paid program.

04 / Transparency

PAYMENTS & PLAYER TERMS.

No checkout, payment provider, or subscription service is connected at this time. If the program is approved and activated, the payment page will clearly display pricing, any recurring-billing terms, cancellation steps, contact options, and the applicable refund policy before any purchase.

Supporter recognition will be optional. No contributor will be asked to disclose personal information publicly. We will provide support for billing questions through our official Discord Ticket Tool process.

Voluntary gifts that provide no benefit in return are separate from these proposed paid supporter packages. This page is a description of the prospective program—not a claim that the paid program has been approved.

05 / Official Rules

COMPLIANCE COMMITMENT.

Beyond the Outbreak will not offer paid supporter packages unless and until the required permissions are in place. We reserve the right to adjust or cancel this proposal to comply with Bohemia Interactive rules, third-party mod licenses, and applicable laws.
