TIPS & TRICKS.

Starter advice while server-specific details are being finalized. Replace these with discoveries and guides after launch.

01

SCOUT BEFORE SETTLING

Spend time learning the map, resource locations, and travel routes before committing to a base.

02

PRIORITIZE ESSENTIALS

Plan your first hours around water, food, warmth, tools, and a way to treat injuries.

03

TRAVEL PREPARED

Keep enough inventory space for discoveries, but bring backups for critical survival items.

04

KNOW THE SERVER RULES

Read the shared Beyond the Outbreak rulebook before building, trading, or interacting with other players.

05

LEARN FROM OTHERS

Trade discoveries and route ideas with the community as everyone learns the map together.

06

WATCH FOR UPDATES

Base restrictions, no raiding, no loot cycling, economy integrity, and player conduct are shared across all servers.
