Nasdara survival manual

TIPS & TRICKS.

Water trucks, rebuildable wells, underground irrigation routes, sandstorms, goggles, heatstroke, base building and the same Terje medical references used on Pripyat.

01

WATER & WELLS

Find water trucks, rebuild damaged wells, carry spare containers and scout underground water routes.

02

HEAT & SUN

Watch for heatstroke, seek shade, hydrate, cool off with water and avoid exhausting yourself in open desert.

03

SANDSTORMS

Wear dust goggles, protect your eyes, shelter from blowing dust and save some water for rinsing.

04

BUILD & REBUILD

Repair mud-and-stone structures, bring materials for construction and plan settlements around water and shelter.

05

TERJE MEDICINE

The full official Medicine and Skills references, symptoms, effects, status icons and treatment guidance.

06

BLOOD COMPATIBILITY

Use the same transfusion compatibility chart as Pripyat before sharing blood supplies.
