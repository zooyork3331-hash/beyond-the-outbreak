01 / Restricted zones

KNOW THE HAZARDS.

Two locations have permanent environmental dangers. Do not treat their boundaries as safe without protective equipment.

HIGH-RISK RADIATION ZONE

REACTOR 4

The area within 1,000 meters of Reactor 4 is radioactive. Exposure can contaminate your body, clothing, weapons and other gear. Avoid this zone until you have protection and a plan to decontaminate.

PERMANENT CONTAMINATION ZONE

BLACK MARKET

The black market is permanently gassed. Expect toxic exposure at all times, not just during temporary strikes. Plan for a functional gas mask with filter and appropriate NBC protection before entering.

IMPORTANT: The 1,000 m reactor distance is the server's configured danger radius, not a promise that radiation ends instantly outside the boundary. Use a dosimeter and avoid unnecessary exposure. The black market is persistently gassed.
02 / Before entering

PROTECT YOURSELF.

Prepare for both radiation and toxic gas. Each threat may require different equipment.

01 / DETECTION

Bring a dosimeter

Use a radiation detector to check environmental levels, your equipment and yourself. Radiation intensity can increase closer to the zone center.

02 / PROTECTION

Wear NBC equipment

Wear proper protection for high-radiation areas. For the gas zone, protect your breathing with a gas mask and suitable filter, plus full protective gear where necessary.

03 / EXIT

Plan your extraction

Choose a route in and out and maintain enough stamina and supplies to retreat. Do not enter if your filter or protection is compromised.

04 / MEDICINE

Carry suitable treatment

Terje medicines address radiation-related effects, but treatment is no substitute for escaping contaminated areas. Check the official medicine and radiation guides.

03 / Coming home

DECONTAMINATE.

Leaving the radiation zone does not necessarily remove contamination accumulated on equipment.

☢

Check your kit

Radiation can accumulate on clothing, weapons, vehicles and carried items. Use available detectors when leaving a hot zone.

≈

Clean contaminated items

Terje radiation allows equipment decontamination with clean water, soap solution or special chemicals, depending on the item and mod settings.

+

Watch symptoms

Weakness, nausea, dizziness and other warning signs can indicate radiation poisoning. Follow the official Terje guidance and seek a safer location promptly.

◇

Use protective containers

Appropriate protective storage can reduce contamination of items inside. Don't mix dirty loot with your emergency medical supplies.

04 / Hostile territory

EXPECT CONTACT.

AJ's Creatures V2 and Solitude Creature Spawn Menu are installed on the server. Stay cautious; individual creature and spawn locations are not guaranteed.

01

Scout before entering

Identify clear routes and places to break line of sight before entering buildings or dense cover.

02

Preserve stamina

Keep enough stamina for an escape instead of sprinting blindly into unfamiliar areas.

03

Bring medical supplies

Terje injuries can take more than one bandage to recover from. Keep a clean kit and backup route.

04

Know when to retreat

When you are outnumbered or dealing with contamination, returning alive is more valuable than any loot.
