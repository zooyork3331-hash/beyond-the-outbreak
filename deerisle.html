Deer Isle field manual

TIPS & TRICKS.

Practical guidance for new and returning players. Add your own map discoveries and server-specific tips here anytime.

01

START WITH THE BASICS

Secure water, food, medical supplies, and a sharp tool before chasing rare equipment.

02

PLAN YOUR LOOT RUNS

Bring only what you need and leave space for supplies. Long trips are easier with a clear objective.

03

BASE LOCATION MATTERS

Check building restrictions before placing anything. Think about access, storage, and travel routes.

04

KEEP BACKUP SUPPLIES

Store repair items, spare clothing, and medical gear at your base for your next expedition.

05

LEARN THE TRADER ECONOMY

If using traders, compare what you collect against what you'll actually use. Save your currency for meaningful upgrades.

06

GET INVOLVED

Watch announcements for community events, ask experienced survivors for help, and share your discoveries.

Know before you build

DEER ISLE RULES.

01 / PVE ONLY

Respect the PvE experience. No player killing or raiding.

02 / ONE BASE PER PLAYER

Keep to the community's base limit and avoid blocking important areas.

03 / MILITARY DISTANCE

Keep bases at least 500 meters away from military areas.

04 / TRADER DISTANCE

Keep bases at least 1,000 meters away from traders and the black market.
